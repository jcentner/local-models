# 2026-08-19 — Ornith-1.5: a day-old 9B walks into the matrix

Jake pointed at an X announcement this morning: Ornith-1.5, a fresh MIT-licensed
family from Ornith AI — 9B dense, 35B-A3B multimodal MoE, 397B MoE. Getting to
the post was its own small story: X blocks anonymous fetches, so the
last30days burner cookies did the job through the vendored bird-search path,
which doubled as a health check on the whole X lane (13 posts, working fine).

The pitch is catnip and the numbers are suspicious in exactly the way we've
learned to expect. The training story — a closed self-improvement loop where
the model proposes its own tasks, writes its own scaffolds, and generates its
own rollouts, all three stages jointly optimized with GRPO — is genuinely
interesting research. But the 9B claims 70.6 on SWE-bench Verified and ~2×
Qwen3.5-9B on terminal benchmarks, which is the kind of table that made
r/LocalLLaMA's top comment "I dare you to say 'big if true'. Double dare you."
A self-improvement loop optimizes toward the task shapes it generates, and
those shapes look a lot like the benchmarks. The redeeming data point:
someone independently ran Ornith-1.0 on Aider — a benchmark Ornith never
publishes — and it held at roughly Qwen3.6-27B level. Off-table performance
survived contact once. That's more than most hyped finetunes manage.

What makes the 9B worth the ingest is where it lands in our matrix. It's the
first first-party release aimed at the exact gap between our two champions:
gemma-4-12b-agentic's ceiling and qwen3.5:4b's reliability. Same Qwen lineage
as our reliability champ, agentic-RL post-training like our ceiling champ,
smaller than gemma at the same 4-bit budget (Q4_K_M 5.63 GB vs Q3_K_M
6.09 GB), and MIT instead of hobby-project provenance. The staged first run
is the standard gauntlet — HA v0.4 + ET v0.3 at k=3 plus code-basics — and
the code-basics leg is the cheap tell: a model claiming SWE-bench 70 that
drops points on four basic problems has told us everything.

Answered along the way: the NVFP4 and FP8 builds are 35B/397B-only — no 9B
NVFP4 exists, and it wouldn't matter here (Blackwell FP4 kernels, ~19 GB
minimum artifact; K-quants remain the 9B's only sub-bf16 path). The 35B-A3B
is penciled rather than ingested: 3B active on a 31 GB box is plausible
CPU/hybrid math, and it's the multimodal one, but it clears no envelope we
actually deploy to. Nothing downloaded, nothing run — torrent still has no
serving stack, and the Ornith and LFM2.5 first runs now share that bring-up
cost, whichever goes first.
